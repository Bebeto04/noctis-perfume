Scope: home route (src/app/page.tsx), the whole NOCTIS campaign. Mode: Persuade (portfolio showcase; visitor should feel the product, then add NOCTIS I to the bag).

Audience/job: portfolio reviewers and luxury-fragrance shoppers; understand NOCTIS I, its notes and craft, then buy. Proof: the six rendered bottle parts supplied by the user. Constraints: no 3D/WebGL; GSAP scrub narrative; one continuous bottle; reduced motion; touch parity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a night-issue fashion advertorial turned by the scroll: every chapter is a double-page spread and the one bottle is the only object crossing the gutter. It refuses the stacked-landing-page arrangement of hero, feature grid, testimonial, CTA band.

OWN-WORLD: Warm black stock (#080706 to #1A120D) lit by a single amber source; ink is cream #F3ECE4, the only metal is brushed gold #C79A55. Didone display set huge and tight (Bodoni Moda, the masthead face of fashion print), a neutral grotesk for captions and controls. Components are print furniture: hairline rules, plate credits, registration crosshairs, caption blocks. No cards, no containers, no glass.

STORY: Visitor meets the bottle, smells the opening notes, falls into the amber liquid, climbs back out to see the object taken apart plate by plate, watches it close, learns its personality, then adds it to the bag.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Bottle dead centre at ~72vh tall over a faint amber halo; NOCTIS as a thin Didone masthead behind the cap; tagline in two lines lower left; EAU DE PARFUM / 100 ML lower right; DISCOVER THE SCENT under the tagline; SCROLL TO ENTER bottom centre. Nav hairline at top with the bag.

FORM: Fashion-magazine perfume advertorial spread, candidate 6 of my list (seed 536ad1a2). Raised by: design annual plate section (registration crosshairs and hairline credit rows as the exploded-view annotation grammar), cutting bench select rail (chapter state is a mark in the folio, not a colour bar), forging (one deliberate beat per part during disassembly). Challengers ink basin (WebGL, banned) and character sheet declined.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
